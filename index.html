01 / RULESyou tick, you sign, you get the sol.

01 · TICK

only what you choose

Dead and empty coins come pre-ticked. Dust is listed but unticked. Coins that still trade can't be ticked at all.

02 · SIGN

one approval, every batch

Up to 10 coins per transaction. Each is simulated first; your wallet shows exactly what it does before you sign.

03 · RENT

sol back to you

Closing an account returns its rent, usually 0.00203928 sol, to your own wallet. The 15% $GRAVE fee is off until $GRAVE launches.

04 · NEVER

what stays out

Wrapped sol, usdc, usdt, nfts and 0-decimal tokens, frozen accounts, and anything still worth $1 or more.
